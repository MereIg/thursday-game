==================================================
CORE CREATIVE CONTRACT
==================================================

MARA begins as a genuinely warm, attractive, funny and polished school/town relationship game.

The horror works because the player first learns to love and trust:

- their bedroom
- routes home
- café
- college
- characters
- music
- phone
- routines
- Mara
- mundane interactions

Then those familiar things become unreliable.

The player should eventually be frightened by:

- three familiar notes
- one chair being wrong
- one missing person at lunch
- a strange blink
- their bedroom lamp being somewhere else
- Mara standing somewhere she could not possibly have reached
- a familiar notification sounding slightly wrong

Do NOT immediately advertise that this is a horror game.

Do NOT make everything dark, bloody, glitchy or red.

Do NOT give Mara a conventional monster transformation.

Do NOT fully explain Mara.

Do NOT give her a surname, concrete biography, reliable family history, clear age, address, species taxonomy or exposition chapter explaining what she is.

Internally, Mara is demonic.

The player never receives a clean answer.

Her nature should emerge through contradiction.

Mara genuinely loves Alex.

Mara is also genuinely evil.

She is not misunderstood, secretly good, redeemable or fixable.

Her affection does not cancel out her cruelty.

Her cruelty does not mean her affection is fake.

SLEEP
==================================================

Do this many times.

For Chapter 1, include at most one substantial sleep-related horror sequence.


SLEEP EVENT IDEA

Late Chapter 1.

Alex chooses Sleep.

Normal fade to black.

After several seconds the bedroom slowly becomes visible again.

The room is extremely dark.

Alex is still lying in bed.

Movement does nothing.

No HUD.

No dialogue UI at first.

Normal room ambience.

Breathing.

Then someone appears at the far edge of the bedroom.

The figure walks toward the bed very slowly.

No loud horror music.

Eventually it becomes recognizable as Mara.

She stands beside Alex.

She speaks normally at first.

Possible dialogue tone:

“You always fall asleep faster here.”

“I remembered it differently.”

Then Mara begins speaking several lines of an invented phonetic language.

IMPORTANT:

This must NOT simply be backwards English.

It should sound structured enough that players believe it might be decipherable.

However, there should be no reliable one-to-one translation.

Reversing the audio should not reveal a hidden English sentence.

Spectrogram analysis should not reveal a simple message.

Do not create fake “ancient demon language” lore.

One line may be displayed conceptually as:

[You understand her.]

Mara then returns to ordinary English.

Possible final line:

“Sorry.”

“Wrong Thursday.”

Cut immediately to black.

Morning.

Normal bedroom.

Normal music.

Mara messages:

“morning :)”

“sleep okay?”

Do not allow Alex to immediately give an exposition speech about the dream.

Keep it ambiguous:

- sleep paralysis
- dream
- Mara entering the room
- supernatural event
- altered memory

Do NOT repeat this type of scene often.

Yeah — that’s already in the game, and I’d absolutely keep expanding it.

Right now each main character has their own dialogue typing profile: different text speed, how often their voice bleep plays, and how long they pause at commas vs full stops. For example, Nia types very quickly, Theo is pretty fast, Iris and Sam are slower, Mara is slower/more deliberate, and narration barely bleeps at all. Mara also gets a special “still” profile where her typing becomes much slower and the punctuation pauses become unnaturally long.

So yes, I’d want every important person to eventually be recognizable even without looking at the portrait.

I’d push it further than it currently does, though. Each character should have a unique combination of:

typing cadence

punctuation pauses

little pitch/timbre of their dialogue bleep

how many characters occur between bleeps

whether they accelerate through excited sentences

whether hesitation creates actual pauses

whether commas feel quick or thoughtful

occasional characteristic sound variation


Like Nia should feel fast and energetic, almost racing through sentences. Theo quick and clipped. Iris slower and dry, with longer deliberate pauses. June slightly uneven because she hesitates then suddenly talks faster. Ren controlled and sparse. Sam relaxed and slow.

And Mara should probably have the most sophisticated one because her typewriter itself can become part of the horror.

Normal happy Mara might type slightly faster than usual.

Embarrassed Mara could type in little bursts:

I was just— pause I mean, I wasn't waiting.

Crying Mara could have irregular long pauses.

Furious Mara could suddenly type way faster than anyone else, barely respecting punctuation.

Then when she becomes still, everything changes:

very slow letters
long punctuation pauses
fewer voice bleeps
eventually no bleeps at all

So you could have been hearing Mara's little voice sound for 15 hours, and then one dialogue box appears where her letters type out in absolute silence.

That would be creepy as fuck without needing any visual effect.

And I’d add this to the Codex brief explicitly: character typewriter/voice profiles should be treated as character identity, not just accessibility settings. The user's text-speed setting can multiply the speed globally, but it shouldn't erase the relative personality differences between characters.
