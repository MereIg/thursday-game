# Reference study — identity, timing, restraint

The user requested lessons from UNDERTALE and OMORI, not copied characters, music, dialogue or an exact jumpscare.

The official [UNDERTALE overview](https://undertale.com/about/) explicitly foregrounds dialogue, humour and meaningful choices. Its published [Papyrus winter dialogue](https://undertale.com/alarmclock/papyrus/) is useful for how ordinary anecdotes reveal affection between characters without everyone announcing their feelings. Applied lesson: mundane conversation should expose habits and relationships, with occasional sincere endings rather than every line becoming a quip.

MARA's implementation uses original per-character synthesized bleeps, different reveal cadences and punctuation pauses. These are our sound/acting decisions; no UNDERTALE sound files, fonts, melodies, dialogue or character designs were imported. Neither the website nor its text is a technical specification for those games' audio engines.

[OMORI's official site](https://www.omori-game.com/en) was located, but the text reader only returned its JavaScript loading shell. No claim is made that this pass audited or reverse-engineered OMORI's animations/audio. The requested broad direction remains small readable sprites, simple limited animation, strong expression silhouettes and familiar rooms that can become subtly wrong. A close frame-by-frame reference study is still pending.

For Mara specifically: retain her own identity and palette; no overt demonic form during normal talk. A tiny positional error, held expression or silence should carry most unease. The existing weather interruption is an original composition and should never become a copied OMORI scene.
