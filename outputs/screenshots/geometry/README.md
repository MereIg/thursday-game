The remaining map-named images show each room's placement overlay. Red is the
solid footprint at ground level, not the object's entire visible silhouette.
Cyan boxes are actor feet; purple outlines are foreground/depth masks; yellow
circles are interaction approaches; orange crosses are furniture attachments.

QA fixtures deliberately set a location/day/relationship. They are not evidence
that all story choices leading to that scene were manually played. Workshop
shapes are technical fixtures, not production furniture art. Portrait-phone
text and full multi-frame action animations remain unfinished.
